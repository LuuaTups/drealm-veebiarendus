### 3. Practical tip: changing websites without losing Google

The most common mistake when switching websites is forgetting the old URLs. Google has learned them over years, and if they suddenly return a 404, visibility drops.

Before launch:
1. List every important old URL
2. Redirect each one with a 301 to the closest new page
3. Carry over titles and valuable content
4. Watch Search Console errors after launch

The full checklist on our blog: https://drealm.ee/en/blog/website-migration-seo-checklist
